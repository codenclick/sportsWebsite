Tournament Rules

Volleyball Tournament Rules

  • Panchayat Unity: All teams must be from one panchayat only
  • Player Eligibility: If any player is from a different panchayat, the entire team will be disqualified
  • Ball Specification: All volleyball games will be played with a leather ball
  • Team Registration: Register before the specified deadline to ensure participation
  • Fair Play: All participants must follow sportsmanship principles and respect officials' decisions

Track & Field Events Rules

  • Age Verification: Participants must meet the category age requirements
  • Registration: All events are free for track and field participants
  • Fair Competition: All races will follow standard athletic guidelines
  • Participation Certificate: All participants will receive participation certificates
  • Safety First: Warm-up and safety guidelines must be followed
